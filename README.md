SQL Exploratory Data Analysis Project

Project Overview

This project demonstrates the use of SQL for exploratory data analysis (EDA) on business data. The objective is to uncover meaningful insights, identify trends, analyze performance metrics, and answer key business questions using SQL Server.

The project covers a wide range of analytical techniques commonly used by Data Analysts, including data exploration, ranking analysis, cumulative analysis, customer segmentation, and performance evaluation.

---

Objectives

- Explore and understand the structure of the dataset.
- Analyze dimensions and measures within the data.
- Identify trends and patterns over time.
- Evaluate customer and product performance.
- Perform ranking and segmentation analysis.
- Generate business insights to support decision-making.

Analysis Performed

Database Exploration

- Examined tables, columns, and dataset structure.
- Identified available business entities and relationships.

Dimension Analysis

- Explored categorical dimensions such as customers, products, and regions.

Date Range Analysis

- Investigated the available time period covered by the dataset.

Measures Analysis

- Evaluated key business metrics and numerical measures.

Magnitude Analysis

- Analyzed sales volumes and performance across different categories.

Ranking Analysis

- Ranked products, customers, and other business entities based on performance metrics.

Change Over Time Analysis

- Measured growth and performance trends across different periods.

Cumulative Analysis

- Calculated running totals and cumulative business performance.

Performance Analysis

- Compared actual results across business dimensions.

Customer Segmentation

- Grouped customers based on purchasing behavior and business value.

Part-to-Whole Analysis

- Determined the contribution of individual categories to overall business performance.
